<h2>What is Zombie Shooter?</h2>
<p>Zombie Shooter is a first-person survival game where you fight off endless waves of zombies. Kill zombies to earn money, spend it to buy new weapons. Collect ammo, health and armor pickups scattered throughout the map and see how long you can last.</p>

<h2>How to Play</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Survive the wave</strong> – eliminate every zombie before they kill you</li>
  <li><strong>Buy Weapons</strong> – spend your earned money to prepare for the next wave</li>
  <li><strong>Repeat</strong> – waves get harder, zombies get faster, manage your money wisely</li>
</ol>
